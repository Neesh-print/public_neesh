For publishers

What does Neesh cost?

A flat 10% on each sale. No listing fee, no monthly fee, no minimums. You set the wholesale price and keep the rest.

Who ships the order?

You do, direct to the retailer, with the address and packing slip in your dashboard. Fulfilment by Neesh is in beta for publishers who would rather send us stock.

When do I get paid?

Weekly, on Fridays, for orders delivered the previous week, straight to your bank through Stripe.

Is my title already in the index?

Probably. Search it, and if it’s there, claim it. Claiming takes a minute and gives you the profile, the analytics and the orders.
